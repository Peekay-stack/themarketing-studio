## 2. Pen portrait (condensed)

She is up at 5:20. The first thing she does with milk is not cook with it — Nanamma's filter coffee has to be on the stool beside her chair by 6:00, and Nanamma reads the milk in that cup: the colour after the decoction goes in, whether the malai came up on the boil. That cup is the daily verdict on Sujatha's kitchen. `[assumed]`

**Head goals** — no second trip to the kirana, the monthly kitchen number flat, Nidhi finishing her glass without a fight. `[segmentation]`
**Heart goal** — to be trusted with the kitchen rather than corrected in it. She does not want permission; she wants the nod to stop being necessary. `[pen-portrait]`
**Challenge** — Nanamma's standard is loose milk from a known dairy. Anything Sujatha has to defend at the table has already cost her more than it saved. `[segmentation]`
**Mealtime** — dinner is the one fixed assembly of the day. All five, and the conversation is the point. She usually starts it. `[segmentation]`

## 3. The hook — locked

**The new poured into the old.**

She never serves from the pack. Milk goes first into a dented brass davara that came from Nanamma's own kitchen, and every cup and glass in the house is poured from that vessel.

**Canonical gesture, unchanged in every execution:**
- Pack in the left hand, held low, tilted; never set down on the table in frame.
- Davara on the counter or cupped in the right hand, thumb hooked under the lip.
- Eyes on the vessel, not on the camera, not on the pack.

**The davara, unchanged in every execution:** brass, roughly 9 cm tall, tarnished dark in the crease near the base, polished bright at the rim where fingers go, one shallow dent left of the lip. It is old and it is clean. Both matter.

## 4. Cast lock

**Never changes** — face and build (early 40s, 5'3", solid, not slim); the coin-sized pressure-cooker burn scar on the inside of the left forearm; thin gold bangles and a black thread, no other jewellery; reading glasses pushed into the hair, never worn on the face; the davara; the gesture in §3.

**Changes freely** — clothing (cotton kurta and leggings in the morning, cotton saree at dinner), hair (clipped up wet early, dry and tied by evening), setting, time of day, who else is in frame, mood.

**Deliberately not idealised.** The scar, the un-slim build and the unsmiling mouth are the specification, not a compromise on it. An idealised generic face is what reads as AI-generated and what a viewer forgets fastest.

**Base casting prompt (studio):** *Indian woman, 42, Hyderabad, 5'3", solid build, cotton kurta, hair clipped up, reading glasses pushed into her hair, thin gold bangles and black thread, small round burn scar inside left forearm, unsmiling and competent, pouring milk from a packet into a dented brass davara tumbler, real apartment kitchen, tube light against grey window daylight.*
**Negative:** *smiling at camera, glamour lighting, slim idealised model, jewellery-ad styling, studio backdrop, glossy skin, saree in the kitchen scene.*
