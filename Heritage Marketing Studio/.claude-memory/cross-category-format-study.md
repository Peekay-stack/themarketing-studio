**LIVE DOCUMENT**: [Format Signal Study](https://claude.ai/artifact/8rFRsLMxuHFXYxKnPtQk11)

Built 23-24 Sep after the user paused Carousel work to ask a bigger question: the product has to serve
categories as diverse as cement, apparel and fintech, not just FMCG dairy — so what does real brand content
actually run on, once you look past the FMCG lens? Instagram blocks unauthenticated profile browsing
entirely (confirmed directly), so every number in this study comes from real screenshots the user supplied,
counted tile-by-tile (a play-triangle icon = video, a stacked-square icon = carousel, no icon = static) —
not sampled, not scraped. Two brands' carousel icons were initially misread as "static" and corrected
before the numbers were trusted.

**The one finding worth remembering without opening the artifact**: carousel usage is a strategy signal,
not a category constant. Cement: 1.4% carousel (3 of 216 posts). Fashion: 28% (and the single largest
format for 2 of 4 brands studied). Beauty: 41% overall, ranging brand-to-brand from 0% (a men's-grooming
comedy channel) to 85% (a science-led skincare brand built around ingredient-transparency arguments that
need multiple slides to make). Video holds steady around 51-57% everywhere — that's the actual constant,
not carousel. **A carousel-first product design would be over-indexing on the smallest share of two of
three categories studied.**

Five cross-cutting patterns found independently in unrelated brands/categories (full detail in the
artifact): deterministic brand-frame compositing beating prompt-repetition for visual consistency;
branded entertainment series as a real, repeatable move (confirmed in 4 unrelated brands, correctly judged
out of scope for a generative tool — it's a production, not a prompt); meme/relatable-comedy carousels as
a distinct genre from narrative storytelling; testimonial/impact-story format with no narrative arc at
all; and an India-specific bridal/occasion content pillar cutting across fashion and beauty.

Ends with 10 prioritised consequences for the app, from structural calls (don't be carousel-first, make
format mix a per-brand setting) down to items confirmed real but explicitly parked (full branded
entertainment). See [[pending-work-list]] for how this fits against the rest of what's queued.
