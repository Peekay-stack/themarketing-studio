Video engine trial

Seven video engines were given the same still frame from the Heritage film and the same instruction, in two scenes. This page lets you watch them side by side. The grades are my reading, so use your own eyes and tell me what you pick.

26 October 2026 · five-second clips · 720p where the engine allows · originals in Downloads\engine-trial

What the trial shows

  • With a good starting frame, every engine kept the real Heritage pouch, with its logo, its smiling-glass artwork and the words "Happy Full Cream Milk". Our storyboard-frame approach is what protects the pack. The engine mostly changes speed, cost and how far the scene drifts.
  • No engine won both scenes on every measure. Kling 4.0 held the pouch colour best in scene A and worst in scene B. Veo went the other way. One clip per engine per scene is a sanity check, not a ranking.
  • MiniMax H3 Max, Kling 3.0 and Veo held both scenes cleanly. MiniMax rendered in 11 seconds. Veo's endpoint ends on 17 November.
  • Gemini Omni was solid in scene A and drifted in scene B. It is Google's replacement for Veo and runs on the key we already have.

How fair is it

  • Same frame and the same prompt, word for word, for every engine in a scene.
  • Omni has "About 5 seconds." added, because it has no duration setting. Veo only makes 4, 6 or 8 second clips, so its clip is 6 seconds. MiniMax's nearest size is 1344 by 768.
  • Wan and MiniMax rewrite prompts by default. That was on in scene A and switched off in scene B.
  • MiniMax, Kling 4.0 and Omni add an audio track on their own. It is stripped on this page.
  • I cannot see fal's bill, so most prices are the published rates, not what these clips cost. Check the fal dashboard for the real spend.

Scene A: the pour

One person, the pouch tilted and pouring into the steel vessel. The camera should drift slowly.

The prompt, the same for allAnimate this frame. The mother keeps pouring the milk from the Heritage pouch into the steel vessel, the milk stream falling steadily; her head and hands move naturally. Camera: a slow, gentle handheld drift, shallow depth of field. Keep the pouch, its label and colours, her face, her saree and the kitchen exactly as in the image. No on-screen text, captions or subtitles.

Scene B: the hold

A harder frame. A different child, a hand holding the pouch front-on, and a glass to pick up. This is the case where a model is tempted to redraw the label or change the room.

The prompt, the same for allAnimate this frame. The boy takes the steel glass of milk from the counter and lifts it; the mother keeps holding the Heritage pouch steady, its front label facing the camera. Camera: a slow, gentle handheld push-in, shallow depth of field. Keep the pouch, its label and colours, the boy's face, his white shirt and grey shorts, and the kitchen exactly as in the image. No on-screen text, captions or subtitles.
__PLAYERS_B__

Omni and Seedance both add a crest to the boy's shirt, visible at the left edge of their pouch strips. Omni also replaces the window and stove with a plain wall.

Reference mode: Kling O1

A different test. Instead of one starting frame, the model was given separate references for the pouch, the mother and the kitchen, and the scene was described in words. The pouch, the pour, the mother and the kitchen all held. The boy's outfit changed, because the prompt only asked to keep his face. Seedance 2.0 refused the same references, saying they may contain likenesses of real people. The prompt for this clip used reference tags, so it is not comparable with the clips above.
