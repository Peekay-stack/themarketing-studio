# Reference-ad learning log

Append-only. One entry per ad, in the order read. Questions, evidence levels and the comparison table are defined in `AD_REFERENCE_FRAMEWORK.md`; read that first. An entry is corrected only by a dated note under it, never rewritten. Started 30 Oct 2026.

## Rules of the log
1. **One ad is a hypothesis; three ads that agree are a pattern; a pattern becomes a proposal; the owner decides.** No rule in the builder changes off one entry.
2. Every statement carries its evidence level (MEASURED, SEEN, READ, INFERRED, OWNER-REPORTED, NOT JUDGED). I cannot hear: sound and music are measured or owner-reported, never described by ear.
3. Views and age are a popularity signal only.
4. The "candidate changes" list records, for each candidate, the ad ids that support it and the ad ids that contradict it, so a change is never argued from the ads that agree alone.
5. Short quotes only (a few words); nothing from an ad is stored or downloaded.

## Patterns (updated as ads arrive; needs 3 ads that agree)
Two ads so far (001, 003), different category, language mix, length and message carrier; **they agree on the points below, so each is at "2 of 3" and none is a pattern yet**:
1. Cut rate at or above the top of our Fast band, with many shots 1.4 s or less.
2. A single place that never feels like a single place (scale, subject and viewpoint change at each cut).
3. The brand is on the screen in the first 2 seconds, not only at the end.
4. Sound is continuous with no silent second inside the film.
5. A visible reversal and a stake in the story (both confirm our concept rules).
Caution: both ads are very widely seen mass-market ads, and the sample is two; a quiet, slow brand film (our house style) would not appear in a list of the most-watched ads, so absence of slow ads here proves nothing about slow ads.
