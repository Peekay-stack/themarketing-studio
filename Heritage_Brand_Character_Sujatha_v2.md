## 2. Pen portrait

**Background** `[pen-portrait]` — grew up in Warangal, came to Hyderabad when she married. B.Com, worked three years in a bank branch before Abhinav was born, has not gone back and has not entirely closed the door on it.

**Before anyone is awake** `[assumed]` — up at 5:20. The first thing she does with milk is not cook with it: Nanamma's filter coffee has to be on the stool beside her chair by 6:00, and Nanamma reads the milk in that cup — the colour after the decoction goes in, whether the malai came up on the boil. That cup is the daily verdict on Sujatha's kitchen. Tiffin follows, Abhinav out at 7:10, Nidhi at 8:15.

**Head goals** `[segmentation]` — no second trip to the kirana; the monthly kitchen number flat; Nidhi finishing her glass without a fight; boards year handled.
**Heart goal** `[pen-portrait]` — to be trusted with the kitchen rather than corrected in it. She does not want permission; she wants the nod to stop being necessary.
**Challenge** `[segmentation]` — Nanamma's standard is loose milk from a known dairy. Anything Sujatha has to defend at the table has already cost her more than it saved. Convenience, value and health are all acceptable — but only wearing tradition's clothes.
**Mealtime** `[segmentation]` — dinner is the one fixed assembly of the day. All five, and the conversation is the point. She usually starts it.

## 3. The hook — Route A (the swap point)

**The unhidden silver streak.**

Recognition sits in how she is styled and how she is groomed, not in a prop:

- **Hair** — shoulder-length, centre-parted, glossy and cared-for. A silver streak roughly a finger wide at the left temple that she has chosen not to colour. Clipped up with a single wooden claw clip in the morning; loose and blow-smoothed by evening.
- **Face** — groomed full brows, kajal on the lower lash line only, and the same brick-red matte lip in every execution. Skin finished, not blurred.
- **Palette, hers alone** — cotton cream and handloom white, brass gold, one brick-red accent. Nothing else enters the frame's wardrobe.
- **Jewellery** — thin gold bangles, a black thread, small brass jhumkas.

**Her recurring prop, not load-bearing:** Nanamma's brass davara — roughly 9 cm, cared-for and undamaged. A soft patina in the base's crease, brass polished bright at the rim where it is held daily — a loved object, not a worn one. No dents, no cracks, no visible damage anywhere on it. Milk goes from the pack into the davara before it reaches any cup. Present in most kitchen frames. Absent frames must still be unmistakably her; that is the point of putting recognition in the styling.

**Vessel negative, always:** dented, cracked, misshapen, tarnished all over, corroded, damaged, rusted.

**Routes considered:** B — the pour into the davara as the load-bearing hook. C — the chin lift, carriage as hook. Selecting either replaces this section only.

## 4. Visual brief

Cast and styled the way a real ambassador is cast — real hair, real makeup, real wardrobe craft, a dewy well-finished glam base — and lit so a viewer is glad she turned up again.

**Setting** — a real Hyderabad apartment: granite counter, steel dabbas in a row, a jeweller's wall calendar, a six-seat dining table with five chairs in use.
**Light** — morning: cool tube light overhead against grey window daylight from the left. Evening: warm overhead mixed with kitchen spill. POS: single large softbox front-left, soft fill right, flat and true-colour.
**Texture** — handloom weave visible, brass rim catching light, skin with visible pores, hair with individual strands where it is clipped up.

## 5. Cast lock

**Never changes** — the face itself: square jaw, high cheekbones, the mole above the left eyebrow, the hairline and centre part, the silver streak at the left temple, the build, the brick-red lip.

**Changes freely** — wardrobe, hairstyle of the day (clipped up, loose, tied), setting, time of day, who else is in frame, framing, and **always mood and expression**. Expression is context, not a trait. Nothing in §4's range is a default for this character.

## 7. Audition turn — run this first

One continuous video turn, framed exactly as this character presenting herself for selection as Heritage's own AI ambassador. A static image can look right and still fall apart the moment a video model has to move it.

> Same woman, waist-up, centred, plain mid-grey seamless backdrop. Single large softbox front-left, soft fill right. She begins in left profile, turns slowly to centre, holds eye contact for one beat with a direct closed smile, then turns slowly to right profile. Six seconds, one continuous take, camera locked off, natural blink. Hair clipped up so the jaw and hairline stay visible throughout.

**Negative:** cuts or cutaways, camera movement, hair falling across the face, exaggerated head tilt, features morphing mid-turn, expression changing mid-turn.

**Fails the turn if** the jaw softens off-centre, the streak wanders or vanishes in profile, or the two profiles read as two different women. A failure here goes back to casting and costs nothing further.
